Our Manufacturing Legacy

ESS ESS ENTERPRISE started in 1989 with a vision to manufacture durable, affordable, and export-quality footwear for global markets. Over the past 37 years, we have grown into a trusted footwear manufacturing partner for leading brands and distributors across multiple countries.

Today, with a dedicated workforce of skilled craftsmen and state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities, we produce high-quality sports shoes, sandals, school shoes, canvas footwear, ladies' belly shoes, and footwear soles for domestic and international markets.

"Quality is not just a standard — it's our foundation. Every pair that leaves our factory carries the commitment of 37 years of craftsmanship."
