OUR MANUFACTURING STORY

37 Years of Footwear Excellence

OUR LEGACY

Manufacturing Quality Footwear Since 1989.

ESS ESS ENTERPRISE started in 1989 with a vision to manufacture durable, affordable, and export-quality footwear for global markets. Founded by Goutam Das, the company has grown into a trusted footwear manufacturing partner for leading brands and distributors across multiple countries.

With over 37 years of experience and a dedicated workforce of skilled craftsmen, we specialize in sports shoes, sandals, school shoes, canvas footwear, ladies' belly shoes, and footwear soles. Our BIS certified facility combines modern machinery with rigorous quality control to deliver consistent excellence.
